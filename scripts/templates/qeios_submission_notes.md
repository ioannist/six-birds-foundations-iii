## Abstract

This paper constructs BirdInt, a finite audited typed calculus for six-bird
interaction. The calculus records finite typed schemas of theory packages,
instruments, visibility and suppression records, witness and update families,
role-channel, directed-cell, pair-observable, promotion, dependency, and no-go
judgments, defect records, statuses, gates, audit records, and model-realization
maps. Every component is finite, and every claim is interpreted under an
explicit host or instrument together with a nonclaim register. The theorem stack
includes no-total-algebra, typed non-collapse, status-family separation,
well-formedness and covered-cell uniqueness, descent and completion results,
graph/cohomology no-drive results, promotion-gate soundness, no-overreading, and
finite rotating-audit theorems. The manuscript also records model-realization
statements for finite stochastic PICA, audited Cantor shells, finite
abstract-interpretation hosts, and finite graph/cohomology supports. Numbered
claims carry concise mechanization footnotes, and a dedicated appendix gives the
paper-to-Lean disclosure table. The paper does not claim a universal exact-six
theorem, a universal six-symbol algebra, a unique decomposition theorem, an
empirical realization claim, or complete same-level self-certification.
