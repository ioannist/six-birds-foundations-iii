# Appendix J. Version History

Version 1 appeared on 11 May 2026 and Version 2 on 2 October 2026. Version 2 keeps every numbered result of Version 1 with its number and name, in the same order; Proposition 35 moved from Section 15 of Version 1 to Appendix F.5, and the limitations and conclusion became Sections 15 and 16.

*Corrected statements.* Theorem 2 asserted a non-implication for every admissible cell; it now exhibits a well-formed $\texttt{action}$ cell with $i\neq j$ and every cell status on $(P_6,P_3)$. Theorem 18 concluded an $\texttt{absent\_with\_record}$ certificate that its hypotheses do not supply; it now states that no positive certificate exists. Theorem 23 now requires the bridge family to contain the claim's own bridges. Theorem 24 stated a dichotomy that ignores higher-priority claim statuses; it now allows them. Theorem 25 now assumes a level above the stack, since the level set is finite, and requires every threshold evaluation reachable from its stack-defect computations to be computed by an $\mathcal R$-program. Theorem 30 separates its fixed-filler and existence clauses and assumes a well-formed square. Theorem 32 distinguishes exactness of a given candidate from filler existence and adds square well-formedness. Model Theorem 29 realized $P_4$ unconditionally; it now requires a recorded refinement at the chosen host whose endpoint closures are distinct. In $\mathsf{CM}_9$ and $\mathsf{CM}_{10}$ the antecedent is a nonempty factorization defect with a complete strict bridge.

*Added hypotheses and strengthened results.* Theorem 3 assumes decidable equality and order on numerical fields and computable threshold data, and Theorem 31 a well-formed, in-scope, visible and audited square. Theorems 1, 4, 5, 8, 15, 17, 19 and 22 have stronger or more explicit conclusions (for example five non-implications in Theorem 5, the lumpability criterion in Theorem 8, and $Z_1(G)=0$ in Theorem 15); Theorems 24, 25 and Model Theorems 29, 33 and 34 drop or weaken hypotheses or add clauses; Theorems 26–28 hold for pre-hosts and arbitrary posets, and Theorems 6, 7 and 10–14 for arbitrary sets and maps. $\mathsf{CM}_6$ now blocks rather than being insufficient. Proposition 35 is a dated validation report with its coverage counts, and the unnumbered statements of Subsection 4.7 are three propositions with proofs.

*Definitions made precise.* Cell well-formedness (eight conditions), coverage, and the cell, claim, pair and promotion classifiers are stated as finite tables with totality rows; bridges, visibility and source defects, instrument admissibility, the model-realization admissibility clauses of Subsubsection 13.1.1, the AI host and the level order are given explicit conditions.

*Additions and removals.* Version 2 expands the reader's map (Subsection 1.4) and adds the Map of Results (Subsection 1.5), the relation to existing work (Subsection 1.6), the vocabulary and symbol tables (Subsections 2.3.4, 2.5), a running example (Subsection 3.8), the classifier tables of Subsubsection 5.6.6, Appendix I, and a rebuilt Appendix F. The claim-strength tags and instrument-indexed judgments were moved from the statements to Appendix I, the negative-scope list of Subsection 1.3 to Subsections 2.4.3 and 15.2, and the final nonclaim block into Subsection 15.2, keeping NC-1 to NC-36. The strength $\texttt{dropped}$ is now $\texttt{excluded}$, and the notations $\mathrm{Def}(f)$, $\mathrm{Rep}_\alpha$ and $\delta_{\mathrm{comp}}$ are now $\mathrm{Definable}(f)$, $\mathrm{Rep}_\gamma$ and $\Delta_3^{\mathrm{comp}}$.
